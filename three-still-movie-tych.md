# Follow2 — Three-Still Movie Tych

## Continuity

- Three sequential still frames presented as one seamless left-to-right 16:9 movie tych.
- Contemporary real-life photographic baseline: authentic bathroom, real materials, natural asymmetry, believable adult human anatomy, ordinary physical lighting, and physically coherent spatial relationships.
- The mother is a living adult woman physically shrunk to exactly 6 inches tall. Her scale changes only her overall physical dimensions; she remains an adult with adult anatomy, identity, clothing, cognition, and biological presence.
- The bathroom, shrinker, fixtures, floor, walls, sink, toilet, tub or shower, cabinets, towels, and every other environmental element remain normal-sized throughout all three frames.
- The shrink is already complete before Frame 1. Do not depict a transformation sequence or intermediate shrinking stages.
- The shrinker's cognitive-control effect is communicated through the mother's behavior and expression, not through visible magical energy, glowing eyes, supernatural effects, or fantasy imagery.
- Under the cognitive effect, the mother willingly and cheerfully walks toward the shrinker, showing genuine adult human movement and a broad, delighted expression. Her behavior is controlled by the premise; do not depict physical restraint, injury, or grotesque expression.
- The person operating or standing beside the shrinker is the normal-sized adult responsible for the device. Keep this person physically separate from the mother's scale and do not introduce additional people.
- Maintain exact scale continuity: mother = 6 inches in every frame; normal-sized person and all bathroom objects retain ordinary dimensions.
- Every frame is one coherent instant. No duplicated bodies, extra limbs, extra fingers, floating objects, impossible intersections, toy-like humans, miniature-room aesthetics, forced perspective, or artificial CGI appearance.

## Tych 1 — The Bathroom

A normal contemporary residential bathroom in daylight or ordinary indoor illumination. The 6-inch-tall adult mother stands on the normal-sized bathroom floor immediately after the shrink has completed. Her ordinary adult anatomy and original clothing remain proportionally correct at exactly 6 inches tall. She looks pleased and relaxed, already under the shrinker's cognitive influence, with a natural adult smile and bright, contented gaze directed toward the shrinker.

The camera is positioned naturally within the bathroom and low enough to make her true physical scale unmistakable without using forced perspective. Normal bathroom fixtures remain genuinely enormous relative to her because they have not changed size. Her feet have believable contact with the floor; her posture, balance, clothing, hair, and expression obey ordinary biomechanics.

## Tych 2 — The Walk

The mother is several natural steps closer to the shrinker, continuing to walk toward it with visible glee. Her movement is an ordinary adult walking gait scaled to her 6-inch physical body: one foot planted, the other advancing, arms moving naturally, torso balanced, and clothing responding to motion.

The shrinker remains in the same physical location and retains its established normal dimensions. The mother does not appear to grow, shrink further, or change proportions. Her cheerful expression is spontaneous-looking and controlled by the established cognitive effect. She is focused on reaching the shrinker and does not recognize the abnormality of her own size.

Preserve the bathroom geometry, lighting, object positions, and scale relationships from Frame 1. The changed position of the mother is caused only by her walking between frames.

## Tych 3 — At the Shrinker

The mother has reached the normal-sized shrinker and stands immediately in front of it at exactly 6 inches tall. She looks up toward the device with unmistakable delight and eager anticipation, still under the cognitive-control effect. Her expression remains naturally human rather than theatrical: genuine pleasure, relaxed facial muscles, ordinary posture, and believable adult body language.

The shrinker remains full-sized and physically tangible. Her small hands and body do not clip through it; she stands on the normal bathroom floor at a physically plausible distance from its base. Her adult proportions remain unchanged except for uniform six-inch scale.

The three frames must read as consecutive moments of one continuous physical event: same adult mother, same clothing, same 6-inch height, same bathroom, same shrinker, same normal-sized environment, with her position progressing naturally toward the device from left to right.
