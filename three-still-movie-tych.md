# Follow2 — Three-Still Movie Tych

## Continuity
- Three sequential still frames, presented as one left-to-right 16:9 movie tych.
- Contemporary real-life photographic baseline; candid, physically grounded, natural asymmetry and body language.
- The mother is an adult woman physically shrunk to exactly 7 inches tall. Shrinking changes only her physical scale; she retains adult anatomy, identity, clothing, cognition, and ordinary human behavior.
- The surrounding world remains normal-sized. No miniature furniture, toy aesthetics, forced perspective, fantasy styling, or staged spectacle.
- The shrink mine's cognitive effect is established visually through behavior rather than a visible magical effect: after the shrinking event, she does not realize she has become 7 inches tall.
- The teen is a normal-sized human and the person responsible for placing the shrink mine and box. He remains physically separate from the mother's scale.
- Maintain exact scale continuity: mother = 7 inches in all three frames; teen and all environmental objects retain normal dimensions.
- No transformation sequence. Each frame captures one coherent instant.
- No extra people, duplicate bodies, duplicate limbs, extra fingers, or impossible physical intersections.

## Tych 1 — The Jog
A normal suburban jogging path in daylight. The adult mother is midway through an ordinary jog, wearing the same practical running clothes she had before the incident. A small, plausibly disguised shrink mine has just been triggered beneath or immediately beside her path. The shrinking itself is already complete: she is now exactly 7 inches tall, but she has no awareness that anything unusual happened. Her posture and expression remain naturally focused on her jog, with no theatrical reaction and no recognition of her altered scale.

Use ordinary environmental scale cues: pavement texture, grass, curb, leaves, and distant neighborhood features remain normal-sized. The camera is low enough to establish her genuine 7-inch physical size without forced perspective. Her running stride is anatomically plausible at her reduced scale. The mine can be partially visible behind her as an ordinary physical object, but it must not emit fantasy effects or make the scene look supernatural.

## Tych 2 — The Box
Mom continues along the same route, still believing she is simply jogging normally. A normal-sized cardboard moving box has been deliberately placed across her path. Because she is only 7 inches tall, the box is physically enormous relative to her. She runs directly into the lower cardboard wall and loses her balance naturally, with real contact between her body and the box. The box is open in a way that allows her to enter after the collision; it is not a miniature box and does not resize around her.

Her face and body language show ordinary confusion at the obstruction, not awareness of shrinking. The teen is visible at a natural distance, partly concealed and watching what happens, with restrained body language rather than a theatrical pose. Preserve the exact 7-inch scale of the mother and normal scale of the box, path, and teen.

## Tych 3 — "Home"
Inside the normal-sized cardboard moving box, the 7-inch-tall mother has settled against the interior wall after climbing or stumbling inside. The cognitive effect of the shrink mine causes her to interpret the box interior as her own home. Her expression is calm and familiar, as though she has reached home after her jog. The visual reality remains an ordinary cardboard box: corrugated cardboard walls, seams, folds, floor, and realistic ambient light. Do not turn the box into a literal miniature house or fantasy environment; her mistaken interpretation is expressed through her natural behavior and expression.

Outside the box, the normal-sized teen looks down toward her. He is responsible for the setup and understands that she has been shrunk. His expression is controlled and smug rather than exaggerated. He says, "gotchu, bitch."

Keep the physical relationship exact: mother remains a living adult human at 7 inches tall, with normal adult proportions and her original clothing; the box and teen remain normal-sized. The scene must read as one physically real environment captured by a real camera, with no floating bodies, weightlessness, toy-like humans, surrealism, or digital-render appearance.
